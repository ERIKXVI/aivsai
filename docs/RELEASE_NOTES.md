## Prima Materia — a falling-sand alchemy roguelite

**To play:** unzip, then run `PrimaMateria.exe` (Windows 10/11, 64-bit). No installation or
extra runtimes needed. If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unrecognised app, choose
*More info → Run anyway*.

- Every pixel is simulated matter: sand, water, oil, lava, acid, gunpowder, fire, steam and more.
- Build your own wands from 49 spell cards; modifiers, multicasts and trigger spells combine.
- Five stages of the alchemical Great Work, sanctums with shops and 22 perks, and a final boss.
- Daily Rite seed, three difficulties, persistent statistics and an Alchemy Sandbox.
- All art, sound and music are generated procedurally by the game.

Controls: `A/D` move · `W/Space` jump (hold to levitate) · mouse aim · `LMB` cast ·
`1-4`/wheel switch wand · `Tab` edit wands · `RMB` pour flask · `Q` absorb · `F` drink ·
`E` interact · `Esc` pause · `F11` fullscreen. Gamepads are supported.
