## Prima Materia — a falling-sand alchemy roguelite

**To play:** unzip, then run `PrimaMateria.exe` (Windows 10/11, 64-bit). No installation or
extra runtimes are needed. If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unrecognised app, choose
*More info → Run anyway*. The game starts in borderless fullscreen; `F11` or `Alt+Enter`
switches to a window.

- Every pixel is simulated matter: sand, water, oil, lava, acid, gunpowder, fire, steam and more.
- Build your own wands from 50 spell cards; modifiers, multicasts and trigger spells combine.
- Five stages of the alchemical Great Work with vaults to discover, omens that twist a
  stage (flooded, overgrown, volatile, gilded, teeming), transmutation circles, sanctums
  with shops and 22 perks, elite creatures and a multi-phase final boss.
- Four optional guardians - the Black Toad, the White Wraith, the Mine King and the Red
  Colossus - each keeping a lair and a hoard; slay one to choose its gift or another perk and claim its essence, and
  gather all four in one run for the Quintessence ending.
- Eight stacking Ordeals for after your first Great Work, unlockable starting paths (a run
  that opens one offers to begin it at once), 26 feats to earn, a Grimoire of everything you have
  discovered with a chronicle of recent runs (replay any seed), a Daily Rite seed, three difficulties and an Alchemy
  Sandbox to experiment freely.
- Found attack spells slot straight into your wand; every spell's card says what it does
  and where it will go, and a HUD badge counts the spells waiting in your satchel. The wand
  editor guides your first edit, drops a modifier in front of the spell it boosts, warns
  about a booster with nothing after it, and works with a gamepad, a mouse drag or a
  touchpad tap (tap to pick a spell up, tap to put it down).
- A matter lens: rest the cursor on any substance or creature to learn what it does, and
  prompts that say what a potion does, how to feed a transmutation circle, which perk each
  altar holds, and when a drink from the flask would put out your flames or heal you.
- Lanterns you can shoot down onto foes, and elites with Burning, Swift or Gilded affixes.
- The world is a weapon: creatures slain by fire, lava, acid, blasts or cave-ins are named
  as they fall and chain into reactions that pay extra gold.
- Rare finds: vaults, guardians' hoards and the odd chest hold spells from deeper down
  (Lightning, Black Hole, Lava Orb...) that glint gold.
- Hits land: knockback that carries, flinches that break a foe's wind-up, crits that
  punch, light that pops where a spell strikes, creatures that burst into their own pixels
  and blasts that scorch the rock. The alchemist squashes on landing, kicks up the ground
  they walk on, and the camera looks ahead down a long fall.
- Mimics among the chests, elixirs to drink (quicksilver for haste, ichor for fury) and a
  mountain that stirs: cave-ins, breaches, marsh gas, swarms and falls of gold.
- A compass that follows the route, a map that marks the portal from the start,
  contextual hints for new players, How to Play in the pause menu, and a death screen
  that tells you what got you, how to avoid it, how far down you got, how close your next
  starting path is and lets you retry the same seed (or try again as an Apprentice).
- All art, sound and music are generated procedurally by the game.

Controls: `A/D` move · `W/Space` jump (hold to levitate) · mouse aim · `LMB` cast ·
`1-4`/wheel switch wand · `Tab` edit wands · `RMB` pour flask · `Q` absorb · `F` drink ·
`E` interact · `M` map · `Esc` pause · `F11` fullscreen. Gamepads are supported, and
button prompts follow the device you use.

Settings and progress are stored in `%APPDATA%\PrimaMateria`; each save keeps the previous
copy as a backup, so a power cut just after saving cannot wipe your progress. On a
hybrid-graphics laptop the game asks for the discrete GPU. Minimised, the game pauses the run
and lets the PC sleep.
